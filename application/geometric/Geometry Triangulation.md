## Define

Geometry triangulation is the process of decomposing a geometric shape into a collection of triangular faces. In this process, a complex polygon, surface, or geometric model is converted into a set of triangles while preserving its original shape and structure as much as possible.

The main purpose of triangulation is to represent geometry in a simpler and more standardized form. Since triangles are the simplest polygonal faces and are always planar. After triangulation, the original geometry can be processed more easily by rendering engines, simulation systems, or mesh algorithms. they are widely used in computer graphics, computational geometry, mesh generation, physics simulation, and 3D rendering.


**Input:**
A geometric shape, polygon, surface, or mesh.

**Output:**
A set of triangular faces representing the original geometry.

**Example Description:**

A quadrilateral face can be split into two triangular faces. Similarly, a complex polygon with many vertices can be divided into multiple triangles.
