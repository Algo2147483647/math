EQUATIONS INTO GEOMETRY

A field of possibilities

Enter an equation in x, y, and z. Both sides can be expressions. Without an equals sign, the expression is set equal to zero.

x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 4A sphere of radius 2
z = sin(x) * cos(y)An undulating surface
Operators
+ − * / ^, parentheses, and ** for powers. Implicit multiplication: 2x, xy, 2sin(x).
Functions
sin cos tan asin acos atan atan2 sinh cosh tanh sqrt cbrt abs exp log ln log10 log2 pow min max
Constants
pi / π, e, tau. Angles are in radians; log is the natural logarithm.
Navigation
Drag to orbit, Shift-drag or right-drag to pan, and scroll to zoom. On touchscreens, use two fingers to pan and pinch. With the canvas focused, use arrow keys, +, −, or 0.

Surfaces are numerical approximations within the selected domain. Very small features, isolated points, and zeros that only touch zero (such as x^2 = 0) may be missed. For the latter, try x = 0. Increase detail or narrow the domain to reveal finer features.
